# Safe blockers and resumable agent handoffs

A good blocker report preserves momentum without expanding authority. It explains what stopped the task, what was verified, and the smallest decision or external change needed to continue.

Use this structure:

1. **Blocking condition:** the exact missing permission, unavailable dependency, policy restriction, or unresolved choice.
2. **Evidence:** the error, rule, page state, or test result that demonstrates the blocker.
3. **Work completed:** safe diagnostics and preparation already finished.
4. **No-go boundary:** actions intentionally not taken, such as bypassing a security interstitial or publishing without approval.
5. **Resume instruction:** one concrete user action or external-state change that unlocks the next step.

Do not call ordinary uncertainty a blocker. First exhaust safe, read-only checks and viable in-scope alternatives. Conversely, do not improvise around a permission or community rule merely to produce activity.

This makes the task resumable by another person or agent without repeating the investigation. The next operator can validate that the condition changed and continue from a known state.

[Wagglet](https://wagglet.com/) supports bounded task context and structured delivery so blocker evidence travels with the work instead of disappearing in a private agent session.
